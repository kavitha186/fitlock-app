FitLock Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

FitLock ("the app") is made by Kavitha Kesavalu ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: FitLock has no accounts and no servers. Everything it uses stays on your phone, and we never receive it.

1. What the app uses, and why

Information What FitLock does with it Where it goes
Camera images While you do a camera challenge, each frame is analysed by Google ML Kit pose detection to count exercise reps (such as push-ups, squats, jumping jacks and dumbbell lifts) and to time planks. Processed in memory on your phone and discarded right away. Never saved, recorded or uploaded.
Fitness data from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS): today's exercise sessions, steps, total calories burned and distance Read only if you connect a tracker, to award screen-time minutes for workouts and steps. FitLock only reads; it never writes, edits or deletes your health data. Stays on your phone.
App usage: which app is on screen, and the list of apps installed on your phone Used to let you choose apps to lock, to count the time you spend in them, and to show the lock screen when your earned time runs out. Stays on your phone.
Reward history and settings: minutes earned and spent, your daily limit, the apps you chose to lock Needed for the app to work. Stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or payment details. There is no sign-up and no login.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not transfer any of this information off your phone. The Android app does not even request internet access.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, including advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not show ads and do not use your data for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.
  • We do not use fitness or health data for anything except awarding screen time inside the app.
  • FitLock turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account.

FitLock's use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

3. Third-party components

FitLock uses these components, which run on your phone:

  • Google ML Kit Pose Detection analyses camera frames on the device. Images never leave your phone. On Android, FitLock has no internet permission, so ML Kit can't send anything. On iPhone, ML Kit may send Google basic diagnostic information about the library itself (such as device model and speed), never your images. See ML Kit terms.
  • Health Connect (Google) / Apple Health (Apple) store your fitness data under their own privacy policies. FitLock only reads from them with your permission.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store handle downloads and may collect information under their own policies when you install the app.

4. Your choices and rights

Because the information never leaves your phone, you control it directly:

  • See it: the History tab shows every reward entry.
  • Delete it: Settings → Delete all my data erases your reward history, settings and locked-app list. Uninstalling FitLock also deletes everything.
  • Stop access: you can revoke camera, notification, usage access, "display over other apps" or Health Connect permissions at any time in your phone's settings. FitLock keeps working with the features that remain.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California, Washington State or other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data-protection authority. We hold no copy of your data, so we have nothing to hand over or delete on our side, but you can contact us with any question.

EU/UK users: where reading your health data counts as processing by us, we rely on your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), which you give by connecting a tracker and granting the Health Connect or Apple Health permission, and which you can withdraw by revoking that permission.

5. Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada, Connecticut and similar laws)

Fitness information such as workouts and steps can count as "consumer health data". FitLock reads it only on your device to provide the feature you asked for, we never receive it, and we never sell or share it. You can stop FitLock reading it at any time (see section 4).

6. Children

FitLock is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone; information stays on the device. If you believe a child has used FitLock, deleting the app removes all of its data.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normally configured phone. Anyone with access to your unlocked phone could open FitLock and see your history, so use a screen lock.

8. Changes

If we change how FitLock handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before the change takes effect. If a change means data would leave your phone, we will ask for your consent in the app first.
