FlexIt Health Disclaimer

Last updated: November 24, 2026

FlexIt is a screen-time and motivation tool, not a medical device.

  • It does not diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition, and it is not a substitute for advice from a doctor, physiotherapist or other qualified professional.
  • The camera rep counter and form checks are estimates. They can miss reps, count wrong ones, or accept poor form. Don't rely on them to keep you safe.
  • Fitness data read from Health Connect or Apple Health comes from other apps and devices. FlexIt doesn't check it for accuracy.
  • Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you are pregnant, are recovering from an injury or surgery, have a heart, lung, joint or other health condition, or haven't exercised for a long time.
  • Exercise in a safe, clear space. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, chest tightness or shortness of breath, and seek medical help if needed.
  • FlexIt's daily limit and credits are not health recommendations about how much screen time or exercise is right for you.

If you think you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

Developer note (keep claims within "general wellness"): in the store listing, screenshots and app text, don't claim FlexIt treats or prevents conditions (for example "cures phone addiction", "treats ADHD", "helps with depression", "prevents obesity"). Medical claims could make the app a regulated medical device (US FDA, EU MDR, UK MHRA) and break Google Play's Health policy. Wellness claims like "build a habit of moving before scrolling" are fine.
